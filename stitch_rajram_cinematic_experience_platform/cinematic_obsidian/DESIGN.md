## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmospheric, cinematic canvas for a premier independent film studio and creative house. Rooted in the visual vocabulary of cinema, modern auteurism, and high-fashion editorial publishing, the aesthetic evokes quiet power, artisanal prestige, and analog reverence.

Key brand tenets include:
- **Atmospheric Intimacy:** The interface acts as a darkened screening room. The UI recedes effortlessly, allowing full-bleed imagery, moving film stills, and typographic compositions to command focus.
- **Audience:** Global cinephiles, festival curators, auteur directors, private investors, and discerning cultural tastemakers.
- **Emotional Signature:** Awe, stillness, deliberate pacing, archival permanence, and understated luxury.
- **Design Philosophy:** Minimalist Editorial with Analog Warmth. Negative space is architectural and generous. Content breathes through wide cinematic aspect ratios (2.39:1 and 16:9), film grain textural overlays, delicate hairline framing, and extreme typographic scale contrasts. Corporate card grids, playful micro-interactions, saturated glows, and colorful gradients are strictly omitted.

## Colors

The palette embraces deep tonal shadows punctuated by warm metallic foil and soft editorial ivory, reflecting 35mm negative prints and golden-hour cinematography.

- **Obsidian (`#09090B`):** The foundational base canvas. A deep, tactile black with an infinitesimal cool undertone that absorbs light and mimics blacked-out theatrical screening spaces.
- **Charcoal (`#1D1C1B`):** The primary surface and structural tier. Provides quiet separation from Obsidian for elevated plates, letterbox panels, modal backdrops, and interactive states.
- **Muted Gold (`#B49A72`):** The primary metallic accent. Emulates aged brass typography, gilt embossing, award laurels, and curated timestamps. Used strictly for high-value focal points, primary interactive states, and active chapter cues.
- **Ivory (`#E9E2D7`):** The editorial voice. A desaturated, unbleached paper tone delivering sharp, legible typography without the harsh, digital glare of pure white.

### Application Roles
- **Background / Canvas:** Pure Obsidian (`#09090B`).
- **Surface Panels / Card Surfaces:** Charcoal (`#1D1C1B`) at full opacity or with 85% opacity over subtle grain.
- **Hairlines & Dividers:** Charcoal lightened to `rgba(233, 226, 215, 0.12)`.
- **Primary Text:** Ivory (`#E9E2D7`).
- **Secondary / Supporting Text:** Ivory muted to `rgba(233, 226, 215, 0.55)`.
- **Accents & Highlights:** Muted Gold (`#B49A72`).

## Typography

Typography acts as the dramatic spine of the brand. High-contrast display serifs echo title sequences and high-luxury editorial covers, set against clinical, highly tracked grotesque sans-serif labels.

- **Headline Display (`Bodoni Moda`):** Used with dramatic vertical presence. High contrast between thick stems and razor-thin serifs creates tension and stature. Hero displays should often be presented in mixed case or italics for director quotes and project names.
- **Body & Technical Specs (`Geist`):** Set at light weights (`300` and `400`) to guarantee high legibility alongside dense media while ensuring zero competition with the display serif.
- **Cinematic Labels & Meta (`Geist Uppercase`):** Micro-labels, production numbers, film formats (e.g., `35MM`, `ASPECT RATIO 2.39:1`), timestamps, and navigation items are uniformly rendered in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.22em` to `0.28em`). This technique mirrors film slate markings and technical negative borders.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors cinematic storyboarding: wide framing, asymmetric alignments, and generous pauses.

### Layout System
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Content conforms to a fluid 12-column layout bounded by an expansive canvas margin (`margin: 4rem`). Elements span asymmetrical units (e.g., 7 columns for media, 4 columns for title/synopsis, 1 column empty breathing pause).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Standardized 8 columns with `2rem` gutters and margins.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Single-column stacked layouts utilizing `gutter-mobile: 1rem` and `margin-mobile: 1.5rem`.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Rhythm Principle:** Large spatial steps (`space-lg`, `space-xl`) separate narrative chapters and film acts. Dense micro-spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) binds technical credits, film slates, and production meta together.
- **Horizontal Sequences:** Curated project reels flow horizontally along variable aspect-ratio frames, eschewing uniform card grids in favor of an uninterrupted filmstrip cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects drop shadows and skeuomorphic light sources in favor of stage lighting, surface stacking, and camera optics:

- **Tonal Stepping:** True depth is established strictly through three core tonal planes:
  1. Base: Obsidian (`#09090B`)
  2. Elevated Surface / Drawer: Charcoal (`#1D1C1B`)
  3. Interactive / Highlight: Charcoal with fine Ivory hairline border
- **Hairline Framing (Ghost Linework):** Boundaries are marked by continuous `1px` lines rendered in `rgba(233, 226, 215, 0.1)`. In hover or active focus, this hairline transitions seamlessly to Muted Gold (`#B49A72`).
- **Optical Texture (Film Grain):** All non-video structural surfaces feature an ultra-fine, low-opacity CSS noise overlay (`opacity: 0.035`) blended with `mix-blend-mode: overlay`, lending tactile analog grain to the pure digital canvas.
- **Atmospheric Dimming:** Inactive background elements reduce opacity to `0.3` during trailer playback or focused stills viewing, mimicking the theatrical transition of house lights dimming.

## Shapes

The design system enforces architectural sharpness (`roundedness: 0`).

- **Geometry:** Every edge, thumbnail wrapper, button container, badge, and dialogue overlay is crisp and unrounded (`0px` corner radius).
- **Cinematic Framing:** Sharp corners mirror standard matte boxes, projected film frames, viewfinders, and photographic plates. Rounded corners soften visual tension and are banned across the system.
- **Letterboxing:** Project covers and embedded players adhere to strict ratio viewboxes: `2.39:1` (Anamorphic), `1.85:1` (Flat), and `4:3` (Archival Academy Ratio), bordered only by precise hairlines.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary CTA:** Sharp Obsidian container with an Ivory border and Ivory tracked uppercase label (`label-cinematic`). On hover, the container inverts smoothly to Muted Gold with Charcoal text (`transition: all 400ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
- **Text Link CTA:** Monospaced or sans uppercase text underlined by a `1px` Muted Gold hairline offset by `6px`. The line expands from left to right on hover.
- **Media Play Action:** Minimal circular or square hairline wireframe containing a solid Muted Gold triangle glyph.

### Filmstrips & Stills (Replaces Standard Cards)
- Rather than standard drop-shadow cards, films and archive stories are housed in pure media containers.
- Information (Film Title, Director, Year, Aspect Ratio) sits strictly outside the media frame in minimal `label-metadata` and `title-editorial` lockups, avoiding floating badges or gradient overlays over the image.
- Image scaling on hover is subtle (max `1.03x` scale with an extended 800ms easing duration).

### Badges & Metadata Chips
- Sharp rectangles with `0px` radius.
- Background: Charcoal (`#1D1C1B`).
- Border: `1px solid rgba(233, 226, 215, 0.15)`.
- Typography: Geist uppercase, `0.6875rem`, letter spacing `0.28em`, Ivory or Muted Gold.

### Input Fields & Search
- Borderless on three sides; grounded exclusively by a `1px` bottom border in `rgba(233, 226, 215, 0.2)`.
- Focus state transforms the bottom border into solid Muted Gold (`#B49A72`).
- Placeholder copy rendered in Bodoni Moda italic or Geist light at `40%` opacity.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Sharp square selectors with zero corner radius.
- Inactive: 1px border in Ivory at 20% opacity.
- Active: Solid Muted Gold fill with a centered micro-square dot in Obsidian.

### Specialized Film Studio Components
- **Timecode Scrubber:** A thin `1px` Muted Gold timeline indicator with mono timecode readout (`00:00:00:00`).
- **Cast & Crew Ledger:** Minimal two-column index list separated by subtle hairline borders. Role appears left-aligned in muted Ivory uppercase; talent name appears right-aligned in Bodoni Moda serif.
- **Viewfinder Reticle:** Interactive corners and center crosshairs overlaid atop full-screen media previews.
