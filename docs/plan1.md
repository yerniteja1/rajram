# Rajram — Detailed Business & Execution Plan

> Source: strategic draft revision consolidated into a detailed plan.
> Status: planning stage — before development.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Rajram is repositioning from a photography marketplace into a **creative services & media production company powered by technology**.

The core hypothesis:

> Customers will pay for a fully managed creative service — one team, one point of contact, privacy-first delivery, quality-checked editing — rather than trying to find and manage individual photographers, editors and videographers themselves.

The strategic recommendation from the original draft:

> Don't start Rajram as a marketplace where photographers and clients find each other.
> Start it as a photography and creative services company powered by technology.
> Later, when we have enough customers and creators, turn it into a marketplace.

This document converts the earlier strategic draft into a detailed plan covering the business model, market opportunity, service offerings, revenue streams, operations, product and technology, launch roadmap, risks and long-term vision.

## 3. The New Business Model

### 3.1 Why managed services instead of a marketplace

The original marketplace model requires two sides — photographers and clients — to grow simultaneously, and both must trust each other. That is a hard problem.

The managed model gives us:

- Control over customer experience.
- Control over pricing and quality.
- Control over delivery and privacy.
- Control over customer relationships.

We acquire clients, manage projects, hire verified freelancers, and deliver the final product under the **Rajram brand**.

Later, with enough customer and creator volume, we convert into a marketplace.

### 3.2 How the business works

```
Customer
    |
    v
Rajram — booking, requirements, pricing, coordination, quality assurance
    |
    v
Verified freelancer network — photographers, videographers, editors
    |
    v
Rajram delivers the final experience — edited photos, videos, reels, albums, digital assets
```

The important part: **customers deal with Rajram, not five different freelancers.**

We become responsible for the outcome.

### 3.3 Brand promise

Customer-facing:

> "You enjoy the moment. We'll take care of capturing it."

Business-facing:

> "Your brand deserves content that never stops working."

## 4. Market Opportunity — Visakhapatnam

### 4.1 Context

Vizag already has established photography studios, so we should not enter by simply claiming we "offer photography".

Known competitive landscape (examples):

- Katch Photography — established wedding and event photography.
- Anand Photography — wedding and family-event coverage.
- 35mmarts — wedding photography and cinematography.
- Cedar Ads — broader advertising and creative production.

Plus hundreds of photographer listings on wedding marketplaces.

### 4.2 The real gap to investigate

Not necessarily a shortage of photographers — rather, whether customers struggle with:

1. Finding a reliable team within their budget.
2. Getting a quick response and transparent quotation.
3. Knowing exactly what will be delivered.
4. Getting photos and videos on time.
5. Trusting that private wedding and family photos will not be misused.
6. Getting professional editing without repeatedly following up.
7. Having one contact person responsible for everything.

These are the problems Rajram should build its brand around.

## 5. Service Verticals

Rajram divides into four business verticals.

### 5.1 Vertical 1 — Weddings & Celebrations (primary launch category)

- Weddings and receptions
- Engagements and pre-wedding shoots
- Haldi, Mehendi and Sangeet
- Birthday parties
- Half saree and naming ceremonies
- Anniversary and family functions

### 5.2 Vertical 2 — Business Content Creation (recurring revenue opportunity)

- Restaurant reels
- Hotel and resort promotions
- Gym and fitness content
- Real estate videos
- Clinic and hospital promotions
- Shop and product photography
- Monthly social media content

### 5.3 Vertical 3 — Professional Media Production

- Product photography
- Corporate events
- Company profile videos
- Interviews and testimonials
- Industrial photography
- Drone footage through qualified operators

### 5.4 Vertical 4 — Editing & Digital Services

- Wedding video editing
- Instagram reels editing
- YouTube editing
- Photo retouching
- Album design
- Short-form promotional videos
- White-label editing for other studios

### 5.5 Launch recommendation

**Start with weddings, family functions and business content creation.**

Do not launch all four verticals simultaneously.

Rationale:

- Wedding bookings provide larger individual transactions.
- Business content provides monthly recurring revenue.
- Editing services can fill gaps between physical shoots.
- The mix reduces dependency on wedding seasons.

## 7. Revenue Streams

### 7.1 Stream A — Managed service packages

We sell packages directly to customers and pay freelancers for execution.

### 7.2 Stream B — Monthly creator subscriptions (business content)

Starter / Growth / Premium plans:

| Plan | Price | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | ₹4,999 / month | 4 short-form reels, 8 edited photographs, basic content planning |
| Growth | ₹9,999 / month | 8 reels, 15 edited photographs, one scheduled shoot session, monthly content calendar |
| Premium | ₹19,999 / month | 12–16 reels, 25 edited photographs, two shoot sessions, promotional videos, priority editing and delivery |

Example economics:

- 15 clients on ₹9,999 plan → ~₹1.50L MRR → ~₹18L ARR.

Note: revenue, not profit. Assumes all 15 clients remain subscribed.

Before selling: define shoot duration, travel limits, revisions, unused credits and posting responsibilities.

### 7.3 Stream C — Editing subscriptions

Editing-as-a-service for photographers outside Rajram:

- Photo selection and colour correction.
- Cinematic wedding films.
- Instagram reels.
- Album design.
- Video highlights.

Rajram sells monthly editing capacity packages.

The photographer keeps the customer relationship; Rajram works as production partner.

Potentially scalable because editors work remotely and no physical team needs to travel.

### 7.4 Stream D — Albums, prints and premium add-ons

Do not stop earning when digital delivery completes.

Offer:

- Premium wedding albums.
- Family photo frames.
- Canvas prints.
- Photo calendars.
- Anniversary highlight videos.
- Additional reels.
- Extra editing and retouching.
- Cloud gallery extensions.

Partner with printing labs instead of purchasing printing equipment.

### 7.5 Stream E — Eventually, the Rajram creator network

Once booking volume exists:

- Freelancers pay for optional premium visibility.
- Editing partners pay for qualified work leads.
- Studios subscribe to crew-management tools.
- Businesses subscribe to content management.
- Other photography companies use Rajram's managed editing service.

Initially: do not charge freelancers to join. Need good creators more than subscription revenue from them.

| --- | --- |
| Customer price | ₹12,999 |

| Videographer | ₹2,500 |

| Travel and logistics | ₹500 |

- Contribution margin: **36.9%**.
- Not net profit: software, administration, refunds, insurance and other overhead still need accounting.

If we can consistently deliver quality, we don't need hundreds of bookings initially.

## 9. Operations — Trust, Privacy, Delivery, Quality

### 9.1 Privacy First

- No unauthorized portfolio publication.
- Explicit client consent before social media use.
- Restricted access to private galleries.
- Secure links and controlled downloads.
- Defined file-retention and deletion policy.

### 9.2 Delivery Assurance

- Booking confirmation immediately.
- Team assigned before the event.
- Defined photo and video delivery deadlines.
- Progress updates.
- Escalation when a deadline is at risk.

### 9.3 Quality Assurance

- Verified freelancer portfolios.
- Test assignments for editors.
- Standard editing guidelines.
- Backup equipment and crew arrangements.
- Final quality inspection before delivery.

### 9.4 One Point of Contact

Customers should not coordinate photographer, editor, videographer and album designer themselves.

Rajram handles the complete process.

### 9.5 Promise discipline

We should never promise absolute privacy or instant delivery unless the operational process supports it.

Make measurable promises and honour them.

Example measurable commitments:

- 48-hour teaser.
- 7-day edited-photo delivery.
- 30-day wedding-film delivery.

Subject to package and event complexity.

## 10. Freelancer Network & Creator Scoring

### 10.1 Initial structure

No full-time hires initially. Small, reliable freelancer network.

| Role | Initial count | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photographers | 5 | Wedding, event, candid, product |
| Videographers | 3 | Wedding films, events, cinematic |
| Video editors | 3 | Reels, films, short-form content |
| Photo editors | 2 | Retouching, colour correction |

13 potential freelance partners, not 13 salaried employees. Some cover multiple skills.

### 10.2 Creator onboarding process

Every creator should go through:

1. Basic identity and contact verification.
2. Portfolio verification.
3. Equipment and technical capability check.
4. Experience and availability check.
5. Paid or test assignment.
6. Agreement covering payment, deadlines, confidentiality and work ownership.
7. Internal performance rating after every project.

### 10.3 Creator score

| Parameter | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Work quality | 35% |
| Delivery punctuality | 25% |
| Professional behaviour | 15% |
| Technical skills | 15% |
| Client feedback | 10% |

Purpose: assign suitable creators to projects instead of picking whoever is available.

Operational idea: maintain two freelancers for every critical skill; if the primary becomes unavailable, use a backup.

## 11. Customer Acquisition Strategy

We don't need expensive advertisements initially. We need relationships.

### 11.1 Channels

| Channel | Strategy |
| --- | --- |
| Wedding planners | Referral partnerships |
| Function halls | Preferred photography partner |
| Makeup artists | Pre-wedding and wedding referrals |
| Restaurants | Monthly content packages |
| Local businesses | Product and promotional shoots |

Also consider: event decorators, bridal boutiques, hotels and real estate agencies.

### 11.2 Rajram Demo Day

Visit 5–10 local businesses and offer a small sample shoot.

Example — restaurant:

- Shoot 3–5 professional food photographs.
- Create one short reel.
- Show the business owner the result.
- Present a monthly content package.

Example — weddings:

- Build three sample portfolios with permission from the people involved.
- Don't give away entire projects for free.
- Sample should be small enough to demonstrate quality without looking cheap.

### 11.3 Positioning line

Instead of:

> "We provide photographers."

Say:

> "Book your complete event photography team. One booking. One team. One point of contact. Quality-checked delivery."

That is a stronger proposition.

## 12. Product & Technology Plan

### 12.1 Scope philosophy

We don't build a massive marketplace immediately.

We build a **business operations platform first**, then expose the customer-facing experience.

### 12.2 Three user types

**Customer Application**

- Browse packages and services.
- Submit event requirements.
- Request quotations.
- Book and pay.
- Track booking status.
- Access private photo galleries.
- Download final deliverables.

**Creator Portal**

- Freelancer profile.
- Skills and portfolio.
- Availability calendar.
- Assigned projects.
- Upload deliverables.
- Track payments.
- Performance history.

**Rajram Admin Dashboard**

- Leads and customers.
- Bookings and quotations.
- Freelancer management.
- Project assignments.
- Payment tracking.
- Editing and quality workflow.
- Delivery management.
- Revenue and expenses.
- Customer feedback.

### 12.3 Proposed navigation

Customer: Home, Services, Packages, My Bookings, My Gallery, Account.

Creator: Dashboard, My Assignments, Availability, Uploads, Earnings, Profile.

Admin: Dashboard, Leads, Customers, Bookings, Creators, Projects, Editing Queue, Quality Control, Deliveries, Payments, Reports, Settings.

### 12.4 Recommended technology stack

| Layer | Technology |
| --- | --- |
| Frontend | Next.js + TypeScript |
| UI | Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui |
| Backend | Node.js + Hono |
| Database | PostgreSQL + Drizzle |
| Authentication | Better Auth |
| Storage | S3-compatible object storage |
| Payments | Razorpay |
| Notifications | WhatsApp Business API / email |
| Deployment | Vercel + VPS |
| Monitoring | Sentry |

Storage rule: use private object storage with signed URLs. Do not store full-resolution wedding images in PostgreSQL or expose public permanent image links.

### 12.5 Core database modules

- users
- customers
- creator_profiles
- creator_skills
- creator_portfolios
- service_categories
- service_packages
- bookings
- booking_requirements
- quotations
- projects
- project_assignments
- deliverables
- quality_reviews
- payments
- creator_payouts
- subscriptions
- customer_consents
- galleries
- notifications
- reviews

### 12.6 Booking vs project

- **Booking** = customer's commercial agreement.
- **Project** = operational work required to fulfil it.

Single wedding bookings may contain multiple events, photographers, editors and deliverables. Keeping the concepts separate saves trouble later.

## 13. Launch Roadmap

### Phase 1 — Business validation (Days 1–15)

- Finalize Rajram branding and positioning.
- Interview 20 potential customers.
- Meet 15 photographers, videographers and editors.
- Understand freelancer charges.
- Finalize three event packages and two business content plans.
- Identify reliable backup creators.

### Phase 2 — Pilot operations (Days 16–30)

- Create Instagram and WhatsApp Business presence.
- Build a simple landing page.
- Prepare portfolios and sample work.
- Acquire first 3–5 paying customers.
- Execute projects through freelancers.
- Record actual costs, delivery time and customer satisfaction.

### Phase 3 — Build Rajram MVP (Days 31–60)

- Admin dashboard.
- Customer booking and quotation flow.
- Freelancer management.
- Project assignments.
- Payment tracking.
- Deliverable upload and private galleries.

### Phase 4 — Growth (Days 61–90)

- Introduce business subscriptions.
- Build creator self-service.
- Introduce referrals.
- Automate booking notifications.
- Expand into more areas around Vizag.
- Review profitability by service category.

> These are proposed targets, not guaranteed milestones.

## 14. Risk Register

| Risk | Solution |
| --- | --- |
| Freelancer cancels | Maintain backup crew |
| Poor-quality delivery | Quality inspection |
| Customer doesn't pay | Advance payment and signed agreement |
| Editing delays | Dedicated editing queue and deadlines |
| Customer data leakage | Private storage and access controls |
| Seasonal wedding demand | Recurring B2B content |
| Low margins | Calculate costs before quotations |
| Freelancer takes customer directly | Transparent agreements and excellent customer experience |
| Too many revisions | Define revision limits in packages |

Additional contractual area: clear contracts around client ownership, creator work ownership, copyright licensing, usage permissions and confidentiality — especially for weddings, where the customer should decide whether their images can be used in Rajram's portfolio.
